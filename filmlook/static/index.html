How it works

Frames of the same scene are grouped. In each group the app suggests a pick (yellow) and says why. You decide: P pick · X reject · U unmark · 1–5 stars · ← → move · Enter next group · ↑ / Shift+Enter previous group · Z zoom to 100% where the pointer is (or double-click; drag or scroll to look around) · Shift+P pick and stay. The strip at the bottom shows every group — click one to go back. Rejects are only a flag — nothing is deleted or moved.
