How it works

Photos you never exported, scored by how much they resemble the photos you did grade, plus a general appeal score. Blurry frames are skipped and only the best frame of each burst is shown. Keep adds a photo to your shortlist; Dismiss hides it for good. Both teach the scoring your taste.
